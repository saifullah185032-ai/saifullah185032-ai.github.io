Open-source AI system

RAGForge

A Retrieval-Augmented Generation system that answers questions about your own documents, with citations, and runs fully on your own machine. It chunks and embeds documents, stores them in a swappable vector store (in-memory or Qdrant), combines semantic and keyword search, and lets a local language model write answers only from the retrieved passages. It includes an evaluation harness that measures retrieval quality, so every change can be judged by numbers.

Terminal Archiver

A background Windows service that collects diagnostics from self-service terminals on a schedule (files, event logs, registry keys, command output, folder inventories) into verified, password-protected archives. It reports its own health, detects hung runs, recovers after crashes or power loss, and keeps running through a missing or bad configuration. Built on .NET 10 with a silent installer for fleet rollout.
